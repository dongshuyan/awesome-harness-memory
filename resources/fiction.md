# Fiction and screen

[Home](../README.md) · [Research questions](../docs/research-questions.md)

Memory settings can suggest concrete questions for system design. The descriptions below use official publisher, studio, or distributor material checked **2026-11-02**. Each connection to AI is an editorial analogy, not a technical or neurological claim. Plot detail is limited to the cited synopsis or excerpt.

## The Housekeeper and the Professor

Yoko Ogawa · Novel · [Publisher page and excerpt](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781250375971/thehousekeeperandtheprofessor/)

The professor's recent memory lasts eighty minutes, while knowledge of mathematics remains available to him.

**Design analogy:** a short span of newly available context can coexist with more durable knowledge. This suggests asking what must be carried forward when recent context expires, and what a new interaction can safely assume is already known. The analogy does not equate model context limits with human memory loss.

## Severance

Television series · [Apple's official synopsis](https://www.apple.com/tv-pr/originals/severance/)

The story separates employees' work memories from their personal memories.

**Design analogy:** information can remain stored while access changes with the active context. This suggests questions about workspaces, user identity, and permissions: who can retrieve a memory, and under which conditions? The setting concerns separated access and identity; it should not be read as a simple example of periodic deletion.

## Memento

Christopher Nolan · Film · [Distributor synopsis in French](https://metrofilms.com/film/memento/)

Leonard relies on photographs, written notes, and tattoos to preserve clues while struggling to retain recent experiences.

**Design analogy:** an external record can carry information into a later decision, but the decision still depends on the record's origin and interpretation. This suggests preserving provenance and a way to correct stored conclusions. It is an interpretation of the film's premise, not a claim that recording more information guarantees reliable action.

Further reading will look beyond science fiction, including unreliable recollection, collective memory, deliberate forgetting, and the burden of retaining every detail. New entries will identify the source passage before drawing a system analogy.
