# Contributing

[Home](README.md)

Suggest a resource with its primary source and a short explanation of what it helps a reader understand or build. Useful suggestions can include systems, formal papers and reports, benchmarks, and memory settings in literature or film.

- Keep descriptions brief and specific. Explain the distinctive mechanism or useful behavior in ordinary language.
- Link to the paper, official documentation, maintainer repository, or original publisher. Identify the relevant version or date when behavior has changed.
- For papers, distinguish the first public release from later venue publication. Mark preprints and author-reported publication status accurately.
- For evaluations, name the task and protocol. Attribute reported results and distinguish them from independently reproduced results.
- For fiction, cite the setting or passage and label the AI connection as an interpretation. Avoid unnecessary spoilers and long quotations.
- Disclose relevant affiliations. Keep unpublished security reports, personal data, and confidential material out of contributions.

There is no required metadata template in this edition. The format will evolve after more examples have been reviewed. Inclusion may be revised when sources change or a resource no longer helps readers.

Entries summarize and link to external work. Linked code, datasets, publications, and media retain their own licenses and terms; inclusion does not grant permission to copy or redistribute them.
